# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing and discovery pages
- Ticketing and event information pages
- Gallery and legal pages that can appear in search
- Crawlability assets such as `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`, and favicon declarations

## Out of scope
- Authenticated admin dashboard (`/admin`)
- Admin-only API routes under `/api/admin/**`
- Internal operational tooling not intended for public search traffic

## Target audience
- People in Mauritius looking for rave events, nightlife experiences, tickets, and VIP/access experiences
- Potential private booking, corporate event, and brand activation clients

## Primary keywords
- After Dark Socials
- AFTR Mauritius
- Mauritius rave events
- Mauritius nightlife events
- event tickets Mauritius
- private event bookings Mauritius
- brand activations Mauritius

## Notes
- The app is a Vite + React SPA served by Express.
- Public routes currently rely on client-side rendering.
